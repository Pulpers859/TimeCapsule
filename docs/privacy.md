# Attic — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 September 2026_

**Attic does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.**

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains what the app
touches and why, so you can check the claim rather than take it on trust.

## What Attic reads

**Your photo library.** Attic asks for photo access so it can find pictures
and videos taken on today's date in previous years. Photos are read from
your device and shown to you. They are never uploaded anywhere.

If you choose **Limited** access, Attic only ever sees the photos you pick.

**Location saved inside a photo.** If a photo has coordinates stored in it
by the camera that took it, Attic can send those coordinates to Apple Maps
to turn them into a place name like "Cupertino, CA". This happens only when
you open a memory. Attic never asks for or uses your *current* location.

**Photo details.** When you open the information panel, Attic reads the
camera settings stored inside that photo — aperture, shutter speed, ISO.
These are read from the file on your device and shown only to you.

## What Attic stores

Settings only, on your device: whether reminders are on and at what time,
your memory range, and any photos, places or albums you have chosen to see
less often. These live in your device's own storage and in a shared area the
Attic widget can read. Nothing leaves your phone.

## What Attic sends

Nothing, with two exceptions, both of which go to Apple and not to us:

1. **Place names.** Coordinates already saved in a photo are sent to Apple
   Maps to get a place name. Apple's privacy policy covers that request.
2. **Purchases.** If you buy Attic Pro, Apple handles the payment. Attic
   never sees your payment details.

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting, no
account, and no server. Attic has no backend at all.

## Sharing a memory

When you share a photo or video from Attic, location information is removed
from the copy that is shared. Where it goes after that is up to the app you
share it with.

## Deleting a memory

Deleting from Attic moves the item to your **Recently Deleted** album in
Photos, exactly as deleting in the Photos app does. Attic cannot permanently
erase anything.

## Children

Attic collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will change with it.
