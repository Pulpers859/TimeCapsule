## Description (4000 char limit)

The first sentence is the only part most people read, so it carries the whole pitch.

```
Attic shows you every photo and video you took on this date in past years. Not a
highlight reel someone picked for you. All of them, grouped by year, oldest to newest.

I built this because my phone kept showing me the same handful of "best" photos and
hiding the rest. The boring ones are the point. A receipt on a kitchen table tells you
more about a day than a sunset does.

WHAT IT DOES

Opens straight to today. Every year you had a camera, in one scroll.

See the rest of any day. Tap through from a memory and you get everything else you shot
that day, not just the parts Attic surfaced.

Tells you where and how. The place a photo was taken, on a map, plus the camera, lens,
aperture, shutter and ISO. The coordinates come from the photo itself. Attic never asks
for your location.

A widget for your home or lock screen, and one reminder a day if you want it.

Share or delete from anywhere. Sharing strips the location out of the copy you send.
Deleting puts things in Recently Deleted, the same as the Photos app does.

Feature less often. If a photo, a place or a whole album keeps coming back and you would
rather it did not, say so once.

WHAT YOU PAY FOR

The free version is not a trial. Your whole history, every year of it, no watermark and
no time limit.

Attic Pro is one purchase, once, and it adds three things:

Recap videos. A day's memories exported as a short film, with slow camera moves and
framing that keeps faces in shot.

A wider net. Some dates just do not have much. Pull in a day either side, or three, and a
thin day fills out.

Your own day boundary. If a night out runs past midnight, those photos belong to the
evening they started in. Set when your day starts and Attic groups them that way.

PRIVACY

Attic has no server. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. Your
photos are read from your phone, shown to you, and that is the end of it. No account,
no upload, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.

Two things leave the app, both to Apple and neither about you: a photo's own coordinates
go to Apple Maps to turn into a place name, and Apple handles the payment if you buy Pro.
That is the whole list.
```

## Notes on claims

- **No price anywhere** in the name, subtitle, keywords or screenshots. Guideline 2.3.7.
  "Pay once" is a model, not a number, and is safe in the promotional text and body.
- **"No subscription" is permanent.** A buyer can verify it on the product page. If a
  subscription is ever added, every one of these surfaces has to change first — guideline
  2.3.1(a) treats a false price claim as grounds for removal.
- **"No upload, no analytics" must stay literally true.** One crash reporter or one
  analytics SDK added later turns this from a selling point into a removal risk, and the
  App Privacy label has to move with it.
- **"Sharing strips the location"** has to hold on every route out — share sheet, AirDrop,
  save to Files, and the recap video, since video containers carry their own location
  metadata.
- **"No ads"** rather than "no ads ever". The promise version is the kind of
  unverifiable claim 2.3.7 bars in a subtitle, and it is a weak differentiator here
  anyway: Apple Photos, Google Photos and most of the small apps in this category do not
  show ads either. It earns one line in the body and nothing more.
